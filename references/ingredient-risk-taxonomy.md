# Ingredient Function And Risk Taxonomy

## Functional Buckets

| Bucket | Typical ingredients | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Humectants | glycerin, hyaluronic acid, sodium hyaluronate, beta-glucan, urea, PCA | Good baseline for dehydrated skin; not enough alone in very dry climates |
| Barrier support | ceramide, cholesterol, fatty acids, panthenol, squalane, shea butter | Judge by formula balance, not one isolated ceramide claim |
| Soothing | panthenol, allantoin, centella, madecassoside, bisabolol, licorice, oat | Useful for redness-prone or compromised skin |
| Brightening | niacinamide, vitamin C derivatives, tranexamic acid, alpha-arbutin, licorice | Avoid promising melasma or medical outcomes |
| Anti-aging | retinol, retinal, peptides, antioxidants, vitamin C, bakuchiol | Retinoids need tolerance and routine conflict checks |
| Acne/oil support | salicylic acid, azelaic acid, niacinamide, zinc PCA, sulfur | Frequency and skin barrier matter more than ingredient presence |
| Exfoliation | glycolic, lactic, mandelic, salicylic, PHA | Check concentration if available, pH if credible, and user tolerance |
| Occlusion/emollience | petrolatum, mineral oil, dimethicone, esters, plant oils, waxes | Helpful for dry skin; may feel heavy for oily/acne-prone users |
| Sunscreen filters | zinc oxide, titanium dioxide, avobenzone, bemotrizinol, etc. | Judge by SPF/PA, region, filters, finish, and reapplication behavior |

## Risk Flags

| Flag | Watch for | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragrance | parfum, fragrance, aroma, essential oils, limonene, linalool, citronellol, geraniol | More relevant for sensitive, allergy-prone, rosacea-prone users |
| Alcohol | alcohol denat., ethanol, SD alcohol | Can improve finish; risky for dry/sensitive or damaged barrier when high in list |
| Essential oils | lavender, citrus oils, tea tree, peppermint, eucalyptus | Potential irritation, especially leave-on products |
| Strong exfoliants | AHA, BHA, PHA, peeling blends | Check routine conflicts and start frequency |
| Retinoids | retinol, retinal, retinyl esters | Avoid casual recommendation for pregnancy/breastfeeding; suggest clinician check |
| Heavy emollients | rich oils, waxes, butters, isopropyl myristate-like esters | Potential mismatch for oily/acne-prone users, not universally comedogenic |
| Preservatives | methylisothiazolinone, formaldehyde releasers, phenoxyethanol, parabens | Do not fearmonger; flag only for sensitivity or leave-on context |
| High active stacking | acids + retinoids + vitamin C + benzoyl peroxide | Risk comes from routine combination and frequency |

## Interpretation Rules

1. Ingredient order is a relative concentration signal, not an exact percentage. Earlier ingredients generally indicate higher proportion than later ingredients within the same INCI/label list.
2. For mainland China products, full ingredient lists are generally in descending order by formula content; ingredients at or below 0.1% may be separately marked as other trace ingredients and may not follow descending order.
3. For US products, ingredients at or below 1% may generally be listed in any order after ingredients above 1%.
4. Use position bands when explaining formulas: `front` (roughly top 10) for base/major support, `middle` for meaningful support, `tail` for low-dose or claim-support ingredients.
5. A star ingredient near the end can still matter if it is effective at low concentration, but marketing claims need skepticism.
6. "Comedogenic" lists are weak evidence. Use them as a flag, then judge formula, user history, and product type.
7. Rinse-off products should not be judged like leave-on products.
8. A product can be objectively well-formulated and still be a poor fit for the user's skin or routine.
9. Do not call an ingredient dangerous unless citing a credible regulatory/safety source and matching the user's exposure context.

## Profile-Specific Biases

| Profile | Prefer | Be cautious with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry/sensitive | barrier lipids, panthenol, bland moisturizers, fragrance-free | high alcohol, acids, essential oils, frequent retinoids |
| Oily/acne-prone | light humectants, niacinamide, salicylic/azelaic support, gel textures | very rich balms, heavy wax/oil feel, aggressive stripping |
| Redness-prone | soothing and barrier support, low-fragrance formulas | fragrance, essential oils, strong exfoliation |
| Hyperpigmentation | sunscreen, niacinamide, vitamin C derivatives, tranexamic acid, arbutin | irritation-heavy brightening stacks |
| Anti-aging beginner | sunscreen, gentle retinoid entry, peptides, antioxidants | high-strength retinoids without tolerance plan |
